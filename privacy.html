Privacy Policy

StudentHub Ghana · Last updated 27 October 2026

StudentHub Ghana helps Ghanaian students find schools, funding, deadlines, study material and student life information. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Operator: Chrisford N Fosu, Ghana. Contact: chrisfordfosu23@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, when you create an account. Your password is handled by Google's sign-in service (Firebase Authentication). We never see or store your password.
  • Academic profile (optional): the university, programme and level you choose to save.
  • Market listings (optional): if you post an item for sale, we store the item details you enter: title, category, price, school, location, description, photos, your phone number and WhatsApp number, and your name as the seller. Listing details, including your name and phone numbers, are visible to other users. Only post contact details you are comfortable sharing publicly.
  • Reports: if you report a listing, we store the reason you give and your user ID, so moderators can review it.
  • Technical information: like any website, the hosting services may record basic information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the service running and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To save your academic profile and show it back to you.
  • To show, manage and remove Market listings and to respond to reports.
  • To keep the app secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising trackers, and the app does not currently use analytics tools.

3. Who we share information with

  • Google Firebase: stores accounts, profiles, listings and reports, and handles sign-in. Data is stored on Google's servers.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's web pages.
  • Google Fonts: loads the app's fonts from Google.
  • Other users: names, listings and contact numbers you post in the Market are public to everyone using the app.

We share information only with these services as needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep information

We keep your account and profile for as long as your account is active. Market listings stay until you or a moderator removes them. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and profile details and your listings within a reasonable time, except where we must keep records by law.

5. Security

Information is sent over secure (HTTPS) connections. Access to stored data is limited by security rules, so users can only change their own profile and listings, and only moderators can remove posts that break our guidelines. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your password private.

6. Your choices and rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Email chrisfordfosu23@gmail.com to make a request. You can also remove your own Market listings at any time.

7. Children

StudentHub Ghana is designed for students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to read this policy with you before creating an account.

8. Use of the information on this app

School details, programme lists, deadlines, scholarship information and study material are collected from public sources and from the schools and organisations named in the app. They can change. Always confirm dates, fees, cut-off points and requirements on the official website before you act.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
